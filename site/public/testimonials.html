Testimonials

Heidi's ABMNM Transformational Movement class was an adjustment for me, as I am used to pushing myself in yoga and stretching, sometimes to injury. She guides you through the concept of small movements, and reminds you if you're going too far or fast. My sciatica is gone after 3 classes, and my hips feel even for the first time in years. I'm moving easier, not overcompensating, and I'm pleasantly surprised when I'm anticipating pain and it's not there!

Linda S.

Heidi creates a friendly, caring and professional atmosphere. The inquiries she had about my issues (neck and shoulder) made me confident she was on a clear path to assessing the approach she wanted to take in addressing my pain. The treatments are seemingly incredibly subtle, but after each session there was a definite increase in neck/shoulder flexibility and decrease in pain.

Paul W.
