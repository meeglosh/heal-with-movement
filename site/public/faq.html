Frequently asked questions

What is a NeuroMovement lesson like?

Lessons are slow, gentle, and exploratory, Heidi guides small, varied movements by hand (or verbally, in group classes) so your nervous system can discover new, easier ways to move. There is no forcing or stretching to a point of pain.

Do I need any experience or equipment?

No experience is necessary. Wear comfortable clothing you can move in. In-person sessions typically take place on a low table; virtual sessions can be done seated, lying down, or standing, depending on the class.

What ages and conditions do you work with?

Heidi works with children with developmental disabilities and special needs, adults recovering from injury or stroke, people with chronic conditions such as Parkinson's and multiple sclerosis, high performers, and anyone seeking greater ease of movement.

How do I book for my child?

Please complete the child intake form before your first session. Heidi reviews every intake ahead of a first lesson with a new child client.
